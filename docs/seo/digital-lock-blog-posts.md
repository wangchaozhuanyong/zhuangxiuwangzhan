**含安装价到底包了什么**
ANZHE 的报价是含安装价，包含拆旧锁、按需修整锁孔、装新锁、为每位使用者录入指纹密码门卡或 App 权限，以及交付前从门内外两侧各测一次上锁与开锁。不包的是额外工序：需要重新开孔、扩孔、门体补强、铁闸改位，这些会在动工前单独报价并书面确认。

**为什么价格从 RM635.20 到 RM2,960**
差价主要来自零件。RM635.20 的 G13 是室内门锁，功能集中在密码进出与房内反锁；RM1,220 的 F8 在入户门上加了把手与动态密码；价位上到 RM1,700 以上开始出现屏幕与可视门铃；RM2,360 的 V37J-P16 有 CNC 精雕机身、隐藏式指纹头与 C 级锁芯；RM2,960 的 P59J PRO 多了广角可视门铃与远程开锁。这些是硬件差价，不是安装费差价。

**门体状况会影响总价吗**
会。同一把锁装在不同门上，工序可能差很多。标准防火木门配标准锁孔，基本是直接替换；老排屋的实木门如果锁孔浅、门边被修薄过，需要重新修孔；窄框铝门的框宽如果不足 38.6 毫米，常规面板装不进去，要改用 D10B 这类窄面板款。这些都不属于「加价」，而是工序不同。

**怎么判断报价是否合理**
先看报价单里有没有写清楚三件事：锁的型号、安装包含哪些工序、额外工序怎么计费。只给一个总价、不写型号和工序范围的，后面容易争议。

## 2. 门型判断篇

**slug**：`condo-door-digital-lock-check`
**内链**：`/zh/lock/d10b/`、`/zh/lock/gp08/`、`/zh/services/digital-lock`

### zh

```
title: 公寓门能不能装电子锁？五个判断点
description: 公寓装电子锁先看五件事：门厚、锁体类型、门框宽度、铁闸间距与门扇是否关得严。本文逐项说明怎么自查。
```

**第一看门厚**
标称 40 毫米的防火门，实际可用厚度常被饰面吃掉几毫米。主流电子锁需要门厚在 38 到 55 毫米之间，超出范围要么装不进，要么锁体吃不住力。量的时候量门扇侧面，不是量门面。

**第二看锁体**
mortise（嵌入门内）与附加式（装在门面）是两套完全不同的做法。公寓大门绝大多数是 mortise，可直接替换；老楼或铁门常见附加式，做法要另外确认。判断方式：开门后看门边有没有一块长条形金属面板，有就是 mortise。

**第三看门框宽度**
这一项决定能不能装。窄框铝门的框边可能只有 30 多毫米，常规面板宽 50 毫米以上，直接超出。ANZHE 里 D10B 是为这种情况做的，面板宽 38.6 毫米，但装之前仍要量框宽确认。

**第四看铁闸间距**
门外如果还有铁闸，铁闸与大门之间的净距决定键盘能不能装在有把手的那一侧，也决定摄像头会不会被横杆挡住。净距小于一掌宽时，带屏幕或摄像头的型号画面基本被挡，改用室内屏款更实际。

**第五看门关得严不严**
这一项最容易被忽略，也最容易出问题。门扇下垂、铰链松动、关门要抬一下才对上锁扣的门，装任何电子锁都会持续报「未上锁」。这类门要先调铰链，必要时补强门框，再装锁。

**自查怎么做**
拍三张照片：整扇门连两侧门框、门边锁舌位置、门外铁闸与大门之间的距离。带上这三张，多数情况能提前判断适配，不必白跑一趟。

### en

```
title: Can a Condo Door Take a Digital Lock? Five Checks
description: Door thickness, lock body type, frame width, gate clearance and whether the door closes flush. Check these five before choosing a digital lock.
```

**Door thickness**
A door nominally 40 mm often has a few millimetres eaten by veneer. Mainstream digital locks want a leaf between roughly 38 and 55 mm. Outside that, the body either will not seat or cannot hold. Measure the door edge, not the face.

**Lock body type**
Mortise and surface-mounted are different construction. Almost all condo entrance doors are mortise and swap directly. Older blocks and steel doors often use surface-mounted bodies that need a different approach. To tell: open the door and look for a long metal plate set into the edge. That is mortise.

**Frame width**
This decides whether it fits at all. A narrow aluminium stile can be 30-something millimetres, while a standard panel is over 50 mm, so it overhangs. D10B exists for that case at 38.6 mm, and the stile still gets measured first.

**Gate clearance**
If there is a steel gate outside, the clear gap to the door decides whether the keypad can sit on the handle side, and whether a camera ends up behind the crossbars. Below about a hand's width, a camera or outdoor screen is pointless and an indoor display is the better answer.

**Does the door close flush?**
The most overlooked and the most common failure. A dropped leaf, loose hinges, or a door you have to lift before it meets the strike will make any digital lock report an open state. Fix the hinges first, reinforce the frame if needed, then install.

**How to self-check**
Send three photos: the whole door with both frame legs, the lock area on the door edge, and the gap between the outer gate and the door. Those three settle most compatibility questions before anyone travels.

## 3. 选型篇

**slug**：`fingerprint-face-palm-vein-which`
**内链**：`/zh/lock/v37j-p16/`、`/zh/lock/a88-face-id/`、`/zh/lock/g53-pro/`

### zh

```
title: 指纹、人脸、掌静脉怎么选？电子锁生物识别对比
description: 三种生物识别各有适用场景。手指常湿或脱皮选掌静脉，双手常拿东西选人脸，家里有长辈可加钥匙扣。本文给出判断依据。
```

**指纹：最普及，也最依赖手指状态**
指纹识别速度最快，成本最低。它的问题在于手指本身：干燥、脱皮、做家务后沾水，识别率都会下降。小孩手指在发育期变化快，半年到一年可能要重录。适合手指状态稳定、日常不太接触水与化学品的人。

**人脸：适合双手常占用的场景**
提东西回家、抱小孩、拎菜的时候，人脸最省事。3D 结构光会判断面部深度，照片与屏幕翻拍难以通过，安全性高于 2D 摄像头方案。限制在环境：门口必须有足够的站立距离，光线太暗或走廊太窄会变慢。铁闸贴得太近、走廊只有一米出头的单位，人脸不是好选择。

**掌静脉：对手指状态最不敏感**
掌静脉读取的是皮下血管分布，不受手指干湿、脱皮、磨损影响。手湿、做家务后、指纹磨损明显的人，掌静脉比指纹稳。代价是价格，掌静脉机型的起步价比纯指纹款高。

**还有两种「不像生物识别」的方式**
密码和门卡。密码不怕手指状态，但会被看到；门卡（或感应钥匙扣）不用记也不用按，适合长辈与小孩，缺点是丢了要单独删除权限。G53 PRO 就是带钥匙扣的款式。

**怎么定**
先问自己三个问题：手指平时干不干净稳不稳定、回家时双手是否常被占用、家里有没有不太会用指纹的长辈。三个答案组合起来，基本能锁定方向。剩下的差别是屏幕尺寸与是否要可视对讲，那是第二层选择。

### en

```
title: Fingerprint, Face or Palm Vein: Choosing a Digital Lock
description: Each biometric suits a different situation. Palm vein copes with wet or worn fingers, face helps when both hands are full, and a key fob suits older family members.
```

**Fingerprint: the most common, the most finger-dependent**
Fingerprint is the fastest and cheapest option. Its weakness is the finger itself: dry skin, peeling, or water after housework all reduce the read rate. Children's fingerprints shift as they grow and may need re-enrolling every six to twelve months. Good for people whose hands are in a stable condition day to day.

**Face: for when both hands are occupied**
Coming home with bags, carrying a child, holding groceries: face is the least effort. Structured-light 3D judges depth, so photos and screen replays are far harder to pass than with 2D cameras. The limits are environmental. It needs standing distance, and it slows in poor light or a very narrow corridor. A unit with a steel gate hard against the door and a corridor barely a metre wide is a poor fit.

**Palm vein: least sensitive to finger condition**
Palm vein reads the vein pattern under the skin, so wet hands, peeling skin and worn fingertips do not interfere. For anyone whose fingerprints read poorly, or who works with their hands, palm vein is more reliable. The cost is price: palm-vein models start higher than fingerprint-only ones.

**Two options that are not biometrics**
A PIN and a card or fob. A PIN does not care about finger condition but can be observed. A fob needs neither memory nor a fingerprint, which suits older family members and children; the trade-off is that a lost fob has to be deleted individually. G53 PRO is the fob model in the range.

**How to decide**
Ask three questions: are my fingers usually dry and intact, are my hands often full when I arrive, and is there anyone at home who struggles with fingerprints. The combination usually settles the direction. What remains is screen size and whether you want video intercom, which is a second-layer decision.

## 4. 铝门篇

**slug**：`aluminium-door-narrow-frame-lock`
**内链**：`/zh/lock/d10b/`、`/zh/services/digital-lock`

### zh

```
title: 铝门与窄框门怎么装电子锁？三点先确认
description: 铝门框边比木门窄，常规电子锁面板会超出或挡住把手。装之前要确认框宽、锁孔与开启方向，窄框门需要 38.6 毫米的窄面板款。
```

**为什么普通电子锁装不上铝门**
木门的框边通常有 60 到 100 毫米可用宽度，铝门框边常见只有 30 到 50 毫米。常规电子锁面板宽 50 毫米以上，装上去要么超出框边悬空，要么直接盖住把手位置。这不是安装技巧问题，是尺寸对不上。

**第一确认：框宽**
用尺量门框内侧到外侧的可用宽度，不是量门扇厚度。面板需要的最小宽度决定能不能装。ANZHE 的 D10B 面板宽 38.6 毫米，是这一项上的下限参考。框宽不足 38.6 毫米，目前没有合适的电子锁方案。

**第二确认：现有锁孔**
铝门上常见的是窄体锁或推拉门锁，锁孔位置与木门的 mortise 孔不同。要确认现有孔位能不能沿用，还是要重新开孔。铝材开孔后无法像木头那样补回，所以这一项必须在动工前决定。

**第三确认：开启方向**
铝门有内开、外开、推拉几种。推拉门不适合装常规电子锁，因为锁舌没有对应的锁扣位置。内开外开则要看面板装在哪一侧，会不会与门框碰撞。

**玻璃铝框门的额外注意**
玻璃门的门框更窄，且要避免在玻璃上受力。这类门只能用窄面板款，且固定点必须落在铝框上，不能靠玻璃承力。现场判断不可省略。

**结论**
铝门装电子锁不是「能不能装」的问题，是「框宽够不够」的问题。先量宽度，再谈型号。只看照片无法确认，这一点必须说清楚。

### en

```
title: Fitting a Digital Lock on an Aluminium or Narrow-Frame Door
description: Aluminium stiles are narrower than timber. Confirm stile width, existing cut-out and swing direction first; narrow frames need a 38.6 mm slim panel.

**Why standard locks do not fit aluminium doors**
A timber stile usually offers 60 to 100 mm of usable width. An aluminium stile is commonly 30 to 50 mm. A standard digital lock panel is over 50 mm wide, so it either overhangs the stile or covers the handle position entirely. This is not an installation technique problem; the dimensions simply do not line up.

**Check one: stile width**
Measure the usable width across the frame, not the leaf thickness. The minimum panel width decides whether the door can take a lock at all. D10B at 38.6 mm marks the lower bound in the ANZHE range. Below 38.6 mm there is currently no sensible digital lock option.

**Check two: the existing cut-out**
Aluminium doors often carry a narrow-body lock or a sliding-door lock, with a cut-out in a different position from a timber mortise. Confirm whether the existing opening can be reused or whether it needs re-cutting. Once aluminium is cut it cannot be patched like timber, so this decision has to be made before work starts.

**Check three: swing direction**
Aluminium doors come inward-opening, outward-opening and sliding. A sliding door is not suitable for a conventional digital lock because there is no strike for the bolt to land in. On hinged doors, the panel side has to be checked against the frame for collision.

**Glazed aluminium frames**
These are narrower still, and the glass must not carry the load. Only slim panels work, and every fixing point has to land on the aluminium frame rather than the glass. On-site assessment is not optional here.

**The short version**
The question is not whether an aluminium door can take a digital lock, but whether the stile is wide enough. Measure first, then talk models. Photos alone cannot settle it.

## 5. 换租客篇

**slug**：`tenant-changeover-digital-lock`
**内链**：`/zh/lock/g13/`、`/zh/lock/g5/`

### zh

```
title: 换租客时电子锁怎么交接？权限清理清单
description: 租客退租后要删的不只是密码。指纹、门卡、App 授权、临时密码与管理员权限都要逐一处理，本文给出交接步骤。
```

**为什么不能只改密码**
电子锁的权限不止密码一种。指纹、门卡、App 授权、临时密码都独立存在，只改密码等于只堵了一扇门，其他入口原租客仍然能进。交接的标准是：锁内只保留业主或管理者的权限，其余全部删除。

**交接五步**
第一步，进入管理员模式，列出当前所有使用者。第二步，逐个删除旧租客的指纹与密码。第三步，删除所有门卡或钥匙扣的授权。第四步，在 App 端移除旧租客的账号绑定与远程权限。第五步，检查有没有遗留的临时密码，全部作废。

**临时密码要单独处理**
临时密码常用于清洁、装修或短租。这类密码如果设置了有效期，到期会自动失效；没设有效期的，必须手动删除。交接时最容易漏的就是这一项。

**管理员权限归谁**
建议留在业主手上。如果单位交给中介打理，给中介普通的用户权限或者临时密码，不要给管理员权限。这样中介离职或更换时，业主不需要重新配置整把锁。

**分租单位怎么做**
每间房一把锁，各自独立设置。退租只处理那一间的使用者，不影响其他房间。G13 这类室内门锁在这类场景里用得最多，因为功能集中在密码进出与房内反锁，够用且成本低。

**交接时顺手检查的三件事**
电池电量、锁体螺丝是否松动、门扇是否还关得严。这三项在换租客时一起确认，能省掉新租客入住后的报修。

### en

```
title: Handing Over a Digital Lock Between Tenants: The Access Checklist
description: Changing the PIN is not enough. Fingerprints, cards, app access, temporary codes and admin rights each need removing. Here is the handover sequence.
```

**Why changing the PIN is not enough**
A digital lock holds several kinds of credential. Fingerprints, cards, app authorisation and temporary codes all exist independently. Changing the PIN closes one door and leaves the rest open. The standard for handover is that only the owner or manager remains in the lock; everything else is deleted.

**Five steps**
First, enter admin mode and list every current user. Second, delete the outgoing tenant's fingerprints and PINs one by one. Third, revoke every card or fob. Fourth, remove the old tenant's app account binding and remote permissions. Fifth, check for leftover temporary codes and invalidate all of them.

**Temporary codes need separate handling**
Temporary codes are used for cleaners, renovation crews and short stays. If an expiry was set, they lapse on their own. If not, they have to be deleted manually. This is the item most often missed at handover.

**Who keeps admin rights**
The owner. If an agent manages the unit, give them ordinary user access or a temporary code, not admin. That way an agent leaving does not force a full reconfiguration of the lock.

**Sublet units**
One lock per room, each configured independently. A room's handover only touches that room's users. G13 is the model most used in this setup, because it covers PIN entry and in-room privacy at low cost without extra hardware.

**Three things to check while you are there**
Battery level, whether the body screws have loosened, and whether the door still closes flush. Confirming those at handover avoids a repair call once the new tenant moves in.

## 6. 断电篇

**slug**：`digital-lock-power-failure`
**内链**：`/zh/lock/g86f-pro/`、`/zh/services/digital-lock`

### zh

```
title: 电子锁断电了怎么办？三条进门路径
description: 电子锁由电池供电，大楼停电不影响开门。真正要准备的是电池耗尽：低电量提醒、USB 应急供电与机械钥匙三条路径。
```

**先分清两种「没电」**
第一种是大楼停电。电子锁用电池，停电不影响开关门，这一种不需要处理。第二种是锁内电池耗尽。这一种才会导致进不去门。很多「电子锁停电就打不开」的说法，说的其实是第二种。

**路径一：低电量提醒**
所有型号都有低电量提醒。提醒出现时锁仍能正常使用一段时间，这是留给你换电池的窗口。看到提醒就安排更换，不要等它彻底没电。

**路径二：USB 应急供电**
多数型号在门外设有 USB 或 USB-C 接口。接上充电宝，锁会临时通电，可以正常开锁进门。这一条不需要拆面板，也不需要工具。G86F-Pro 就是主打这条路径的款式。

**路径三：机械钥匙**
每把锁都配机械钥匙，走的是纯机械路径，完全不受电量影响。钥匙孔通常在面板底部或隐藏在盖板下。这一条最可靠，前提是钥匙放在屋外能拿到的地方，而不是锁在屋里。

**三件应该提前做的事**
第一，把机械钥匙放在屋外可靠位置，或者交给一位可信的邻居。第二，家里常备一组对应型号的电池。第三，交付时让每位使用者亲手操作一次应急供电，看过说明书和亲手做过是两回事。

**关于充电款**
带屏幕与摄像头的型号多用充电电池。充电时锁仍可正常使用，不需要拆下来。交付时会说明充电口位置、大概充电时长，以及充电期间哪些功能会暂停。

title: What Happens When a Digital Lock Loses Power
description: A building blackout does not affect a battery-powered lock. What matters is a flat battery: low-battery alerts, USB emergency power and the mechanical key.
```

**Two different kinds of "no power"**
The first is a building blackout. The lock runs on its own battery, so nothing changes and there is nothing to do. The second is a flat battery inside the lock. That is the one that locks you out. Most claims that "digital locks fail in a blackout" are really describing the second case.

**Path one: the low-battery alert**
Every model warns before the battery dies. The lock keeps working for a period after the alert appears, which is your window to replace the battery. Act on the alert rather than waiting for it to fail.

**Path two: USB emergency power**
Most models put a USB or USB-C port on the outside. Connect a power bank and the lock powers up temporarily so you can open it normally. No panel removal, no tools. G86F-Pro is built around this path.

**Path three: the mechanical key**
Every lock ships with a mechanical key on a purely mechanical path, unaffected by battery state. The keyway usually sits at the bottom of the panel or under a cover. This is the most reliable route, provided the key is somewhere outside the door rather than locked inside the house.

**Three things to arrange in advance**
First, keep the mechanical key somewhere accessible outside, or with a trusted neighbour. Second, keep a spare set of the correct battery type at home. Third, have every user physically try the emergency power at handover. Reading the manual and having done it once are different things.

**About rechargeable models**
Screen and camera models mostly use rechargeable batteries. The lock stays usable while charging and does not need removing. The charging port location, approximate charging time and which features pause during charging are explained at handover.
