<p>Ara Damansara is a planned township in Petaling Jaya, developed mainly from the 2000s, and its housing is overwhelmingly high-rise. Condominiums and serviced apartments sit above commercial podiums, with the LRT station and the airport flight path shaping both the layout of the blocks and the noise profile of individual units. Because the buildings are young, the work here is rarely about replacing failed systems — it is about layout, storage, acoustics and electrical capacity.</p>
<p><strong>What tends to come up in Ara Damansara</strong></p>
<p>The recurring requests are kitchens that need more power and better extraction than the original specification allowed, bathrooms that need improved ventilation because many units cannot cross-ventilate, storage that the compact layout never provided, and window treatments or secondary glazing for units facing the flight path or a busy road. Electrical additions are common, and newer buildings often have tighter circuit allocation, so adding several air-conditioning units or an induction hob without checking capacity is how breakers start tripping. Water pressure on upper floors is worth checking before specifying a rain shower or instant heater.</p>
<p><strong>Management and access</strong></p>
<p>Most blocks are managed by a JMB or MC with a formal renovation application, deposit, contractor registration and fixed working hours, and many require lift lobby and corridor protection before materials arrive. Because a high proportion of units are rented, some managements require the owner's written consent before a tenant renovates — worth confirming early if the unit is not owner-occupied.</p>
<p><strong>Typical scope</strong></p>
<p>For a condominium or serviced apartment: kitchen and bathroom renovation, built-in wardrobes and cabinets, flooring, lighting and electrical additions, air-conditioning relocation, and where noise is an issue, window and door sealing. In most units the electrical upgrade and the ventilation strategy decide what else is possible, because without capacity and airflow the appliance and layout plans cannot both be delivered.</p>
<p><strong>Quotation and handover</strong></p>

<p>雪兰莪环绕吉隆坡，下辖九个县，这里的装修不是一件事，而是好几件。巴生的排屋、梳邦再也的公寓、无拉港的厂房，归不同地方政府管，建于不同年代，装修的原因也不一样。这一页讲的是我们在全州怎么作业，而不是描述某一个街区——因为任何雪兰莪项目的第一问题都是：归哪个部门管、属于哪类建筑。</p>
<p><strong>你的地址归哪个部门</strong></p>
<p>雪兰莪有多个地方政府，送审流程各不相同：MBSA 管莎阿南，MBPJ 管八打灵再也，MBSJ 管梳邦再也及周边部分县区，MPKj 管加影，MPK 管巴生，MPSepang 管雪邦。各自的表格、图纸要求与处理时间都不同。画图之前先确认正确的部门，是开头最有用的一步，因为送错单位的代价是时间与费用一起浪费。</p>
<p><strong>不同走廊的房产结构不同</strong></p>
<p>老走廊——巴生、八打灵再也的 SS 区、加影部分地区——是 1970 至 1990 年代的排屋与店屋，工程多为重新拉电、换水管、屋顶修补与浴室重做。新走廊——实达阿南、哥打白沙罗、赛城、士毛月、万挠——是 2000 年代之后的排屋与公寓，工程多为格局、收纳与扩建。两类都要早查电容量，因为它决定这户人实际能跑多少电器。</p>
<p><strong>工业与商业工程</strong></p>
<p>巴生谷的工业存量很大一部分在雪兰莪，尤其集中在巴生、莎阿南、无拉港与蒲种一带。厂房与仓库工程看的是地面承载、通风、电容量，以及如何围绕不能停的生产来分期。这与住宅是完全不同的任务书，通常从运营对话开始，而不是从设计开始。</p>
<p><strong>报价与交付</strong></p>
<p>任何雪兰莪项目，报价都会写明适用哪个地方政府送审、由谁办理，以及哪些审批归大楼或社区管理。交付时测试插座与龙头、浴室按日常使用试、扩建交接处与排水路径检查渗水、缺陷书面记录并约定返场日期。州内往返距离同时影响工期与成本，所以到场次数会写在报价里，而不是中途才发现。</p>
