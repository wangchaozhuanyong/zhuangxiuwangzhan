## 一、报价与流程（1–5）

**1. Do you provide free quotations?**
```
Yes. We measure on site and issue an itemised quotation, not a lump sum. The quotation separates confirmed work, provisional items that can only be assessed after opening up, and anything you plan to supply yourself.
```

**中文**：提供。我们会现场测量并出具分项报价，而不是一个总价。报价单会把已确认工程、需要打开后才能判断的暂定项目，以及你打算自购的部分分开列清。

---

**2. What is included in the quotation, and what is not?**
```
Included: labour, the materials listed, removal of debris, and the coordination described. Not included unless stated: owner-supplied items, submissions to authorities, building management deposits, and third-party work such as air-conditioning installation by the equipment supplier.
```

**中文**：包含人工、列明的材料、废料清运，以及报价中写明的协调工作。不包含（除非另外写明）：业主自购项目、政府送审、管理处押金，以及第三方工程（例如由电器供应商自行安装的空调）。

---

**3. Why does the quotation contain provisional items?**
```
Because some conditions can only be seen after demolition. A raised bathroom floor, a corroded pipe behind tiles, or a wall that turns out to be structural are common examples. Those items are marked as provisional with a stated approach, and we confirm before the additional work proceeds rather than billing afterwards.
```

**中文**：因为有些状况要拆开之后才看得见。浴室地面被抬高过、瓷砖背后的管道锈蚀、一堵墙其实是结构墙，都是常见例子。这类项目会标为暂定并写明处理方式，追加之前先确认，而不是做完再算。

---

**4. Can you quote from drawings or photos without a site visit?**
```
We can give an indicative range from drawings or photos, but not a firm quotation. Door thickness, substrate condition, access and management requirements all change the number, and none of them can be judged from a photograph.
```

**中文**：可以按图纸或照片给一个参考区间，但不会给确定报价。门厚、基层状况、进场条件与管理处要求都会改变数字，而这些都无法只看照片判断。

---

**5. How long does it take from enquiry to start?**
```
It depends on scope and on approvals. A straightforward renovation can usually move from confirmed quotation to start within {占位} weeks. Anything requiring local authority submission or building management approval takes longer, and that waiting time should be planned for rather than discovered.
```

**中文**：取决于工程范围与审批。一般规模的装修，从确认报价到开工通常需要 {占位} 周。涉及地方政府送审或管理处审批的会更久，这段时间应该提前规划，而不是等发现。

## 二、收费与付款（6–8）

**6. How are payments structured?**
```
Payments follow progress, not a single upfront sum. The schedule is stated in the quotation and tied to defined milestones, so each payment corresponds to work that has actually been completed and can be inspected.
```

**中文**：按进度付款，不是一次预付。付款节点写在报价单里，与明确的里程碑挂钩，每一笔都对应已经完成、可以检查的工程。

---

**7. Do you require a deposit?**
```
Yes, a deposit is required to confirm the schedule and to order materials. The amount and the refund conditions are stated in the quotation, and the balance is tied to progress milestones.
```

**中文**：需要，定金用于确认排期与订购材料。金额与退还条件会写进报价单，余款按进度节点支付。

---

**8. Are there costs I should expect beyond the quotation?**
```
Three things commonly sit outside a renovation quotation: building management deposits (refundable, but cash flow), authority submission fees, and appliances or fittings you supply yourself. Ask for these to be listed separately so there are no surprises.
```

**中文**：有三项常在装修报价之外：管理处押金（可退，但占用现金）、政府送审费用，以及你自己采购的电器或洁具。请要求把这三项单独列出，避免中途意外。

## 三、工期与居住（9–12）

**9. How long does a renovation take?**
```
A kitchen or bathroom typically takes {占位} to {占位} weeks. A full unit takes {占位} to {占位} weeks. A landed house with extensions takes longer, and the approval period sits before the work rather than inside it.
```

**中文**：厨房或浴室通常需要 {占位} 至 {占位} 周。整间单位需要 {占位} 至 {占位} 周。有扩建的有地住宅更久，而且审批期是在施工之前，不算在施工期内。

---

**10. What causes delays?**
```
Four things, in order of frequency: management approval taking longer than expected, changes requested after work has started, materials arriving late, and weather for exterior work. The first two are the ones you control most directly.
```

**中文**：四件事，按频率排：管理处审批比预期久、开工后追加变更、材料到货延迟、外墙工程遇到天气。前两项是你最能直接控制的。

---

**11. Can we live in the unit during the renovation?**
```
For partial work, usually yes, if the rooms are done in sequence and one bathroom and the kitchen stay usable. For full renovations affecting services throughout the unit, we recommend temporary accommodation, because water and power shutdowns make daily life impractical.
```

**中文**：局部工程通常可以，前提是按房间分区施工、保留一间浴室与厨房可用。涉及全屋水电的全屋装修，我们建议暂时外住，因为停水停电会让日常生活无法维持。

---

**12. Can work be done at night or on weekends?**
```
In landed homes, sometimes, subject to local noise rules. In condominiums and offices, almost never — most buildings restrict work to weekday daytime hours, and some ban noisy work entirely during exam periods or public holidays.
```

**中文**：有地住宅有时可以，但要遵守当地噪音规定。公寓与办公室基本不行——多数大楼只允许工作日白天施工，部分在考试期或公共假期完全禁止噪音工序。

## 四、管理处与审批（13–16）

**13. Do I need approval to renovate my condo?**
```
Almost always. Most buildings require a renovation application, contractor registration, worker list, insurance and a deposit before work starts. Some also require drawings for anything affecting partitions or services. Confirm the requirements for your specific building rather than assuming they match a neighbour's.
```

**中文**：几乎都需要。多数大楼要求装修申请、承包商登记、工人名单、保险与押金，才能开工。部分还要求提交图纸（涉及隔间或机电时）。请确认你所在大楼的具体要求，不要假设和邻居的一样。

---

**14. Who pays the management deposit?**
```
The owner, normally, and it is refundable after inspection. It is worth confirming the refund conditions before work starts — some buildings deduct for common-area damage, late waste removal or work outside permitted hours.
```

**中文**：通常由业主支付，验收后可退。开工前值得先确认退还条件——部分大楼会因公共区域损伤、废料清运超时或超时施工而扣款。

---

**15. Do you handle local authority submissions?**
```
Yes for the drawing and submission scope stated in the quotation. Anything requiring professional certification — structural, fire or M&E engineering — is identified before work starts, along with who is responsible for it.
```

**中文**：报价中写明的图纸与送审范围由我们处理。需要专业认证的部分——结构、消防或机电工程——会在开工前指出，并写明由谁负责。

---

**16. Can I remove a structural wall?**
```
Only with engineering input and approval. A wall that looks non-structural may still carry load, and a beam may be required where one is removed. Opening a structural wall without approval risks a reinstatement order and, more importantly, the safety of the building.
```

**中文**：必须经过结构工程评估与审批。看起来不是结构的墙也可能承重，拆掉之后可能需要加梁。未经批准拆结构墙，可能被要求复原，更重要的是影响整栋楼的安全。

## 五、保修与售后（17–19）

**17. Do you provide warranty?**
```
Yes, with the scope and duration stated in the quotation or project document rather than promised verbally. Defects reported within the agreed period are inspected and rectified.
```

**中文**：有，保修范围与期限会写进报价单或项目文件，而不是口头承诺。在约定期限内提出的缺陷，我们会检查并处理。

---

**18. What is not covered by warranty?**
```
Typically: damage caused by misuse, work by other parties after handover, normal settlement cracks, and items supplied by the owner or by third parties — those follow their own manufacturer terms.
```

**中文**：通常不包含：使用不当造成的损坏、交付后他方施工造成的损坏、正常的沉降裂缝，以及业主自购或第三方提供的项目——那些按厂商自身条款处理。

---

**19. Who do I contact if something goes wrong after handover?**
```
The same contact who handled your project. Report the issue with a photo and a description of when it appeared; we will arrange an inspection and confirm in writing what will be done and when.
```

**中文**：联系当初跟你对接的同一个人。请附照片并说明问题是什么时候出现的；我们会安排检查，并书面确认要做什么、什么时候做。

## 六、材料与选择（20–23）

**20. Can I supply my own materials?**
```
Yes. When you supply, the quotation covers installation only, and compatibility is confirmed before the work is scheduled. Keep spare pieces for tiles and laminates, because matching a discontinued batch later is difficult.
```

**中文**：可以。你自购时，报价只含安装，兼容性会在排期前确认。瓷砖与饰面板请预留备用，因为停产后再配同批次很难。

---

**21. Can I see samples before deciding?**
```
Yes, and we recommend it. Colour on a screen is not reliable, and finish — matt versus gloss, veneer versus laminate — reads very differently in daylight. Confirm the sample before panels are cut, because changing a finish afterwards is slow and expensive.
```

**中文**：可以，而且建议这样做。屏幕上的颜色不可靠，而且饰面——哑光与亮光、木皮与三聚氰胺——在自然光下差别很大。请在开料前确认样板，因为之后再改既慢又贵。

---

**22. Can I specify a particular brand?**
```
Yes, and it is often the right call for appliances and hardware. For materials that need installation skill — waterproofing systems, adhesives, coatings — confirm that the specified product is one the installer is trained on, because a good product applied wrongly fails anyway.
```

**中文**：可以，电器与五金通常就该指定品牌。但对于需要施工技术的材料——防水系统、粘结剂、涂料——请确认指定产品是施工方受训过的，因为好材料用错工法一样会失败。

---

**23. Do you work with my existing furniture or fittings?**
```
Yes. Mark what stays before pricing demolition, so the new layout is checked against those fixed points. Keeping a wardrobe or a worktop usually saves money, but only if the new plan actually fits around it.
```

**中文**：可以。报价拆除之前先标出要保留的部分，让新布局按这些固定点核对。保留衣柜或台面通常能省钱，但前提是新方案真的绕得开。
